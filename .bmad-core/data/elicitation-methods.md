# Elicitation Methods

A comprehensive collection of methods for deepening analysis, exploring alternatives, and enhancing content quality through structured elicitation techniques.

## Core Analysis Methods

### 1. Expand or Contract for Audience
**Purpose**: Adjust content depth and complexity for different audiences
**Process**: 
- Identify target audience's expertise level
- Expand technical details for expert audiences
- Simplify and add context for general audiences
- Adjust terminology and examples appropriately
**When to Use**: Content needs to serve multiple audiences or current level is unclear

### 2. Critique and Refine
**Purpose**: Identify weaknesses and improvement opportunities
**Process**:
- Apply critical lens to current content
- Identify gaps, inconsistencies, or unclear areas
- Suggest specific improvements
- Validate assumptions and claims
**When to Use**: Content appears complete but needs quality enhancement

### 3. Identify Potential Risks
**Purpose**: Surface risks and challenges not yet considered
**Process**:
- Systematically examine each aspect for potential risks
- Consider technical, business, operational, and market risks
- Assess probability and impact of each risk
- Suggest mitigation strategies
**When to Use**: High-stakes decisions or complex implementations

### 4. Assess Alignment with Goals
**Purpose**: Ensure content supports stated objectives
**Process**:
- Compare content against stated goals and success criteria
- Identify misalignments or gaps
- Suggest adjustments to improve goal alignment
- Validate that approach serves intended outcomes
**When to Use**: Strategic decisions or when goals are clearly defined

## Technical Analysis Methods

### 5. Tree of Thoughts
**Purpose**: Explore multiple reasoning paths systematically
**Process**:
- Break down complex problems into smaller components
- Generate multiple solution approaches for each component
- Evaluate and combine the best elements
- Build comprehensive solution from proven parts
**When to Use**: Complex technical problems with multiple solution paths

### 6. ReWOO (Reasoning WithOut Observation)
**Purpose**: Structured multi-step reasoning without external validation
**Process**:
- Plan reasoning steps in advance
- Execute each step systematically
- Solve sub-problems independently
- Combine results into final solution
**When to Use**: Problems requiring systematic logical reasoning

### 7. Meta-Prompting
**Purpose**: Improve analysis through self-reflection on reasoning process
**Process**:
- Examine the reasoning approach being used
- Identify potential biases or blind spots
- Consider alternative analysis methods
- Refine approach based on meta-analysis
**When to Use**: Complex analysis where methodology matters

## Stakeholder Perspective Methods

### 8. Agile Team Perspective
**Purpose**: Evaluate from software development team viewpoint
**Process**:
- Consider development velocity and sprint planning
- Assess technical debt and maintainability implications
- Evaluate testing and quality assurance needs
- Consider deployment and operational impacts
**When to Use**: Software development projects or technical implementations

### 9. Stakeholder Roundtable
**Purpose**: Examine from multiple stakeholder perspectives
**Process**:
- Identify all relevant stakeholders
- Consider each stakeholder's needs and concerns
- Explore potential conflicts or synergies
- Find solutions that balance different interests
**When to Use**: Decisions affecting multiple groups or organizational initiatives

## Creative Exploration Methods

### 10. Innovation Tournament
**Purpose**: Generate competitive alternatives through structured competition
**Process**:
- Create multiple solution alternatives
- Define evaluation criteria
- Conduct systematic comparison
- Select winning elements from each alternative
**When to Use**: Need breakthrough solutions or challenging status quo

### 11. Escape Room Challenge
**Purpose**: Find creative solutions under artificial constraints
**Process**:
- Add artificial constraints or limitations
- Force creative problem-solving within constraints
- Remove constraints and see which solutions still work
- Identify unexpectedly elegant solutions
**When to Use**: Breaking out of conventional thinking patterns

## Strategic Analysis Methods

### 12. Red Team vs Blue Team
**Purpose**: Adversarial analysis to stress-test ideas
**Process**:
- Blue team develops and defends proposal
- Red team attacks proposal looking for weaknesses
- Both teams learn from the exercise
- Strengthen proposal based on adversarial feedback
**When to Use**: High-stakes decisions or security-critical implementations

### 13. Hindsight Reflection
**Purpose**: Learn from imagined future outcomes
**Process**:
- Imagine project has succeeded brilliantly
- Identify what factors led to success
- Imagine project has failed completely
- Identify what factors led to failure
- Adjust current approach based on insights
**When to Use**: Strategic planning or major initiative launches

## Validation and Testing Methods

### 14. Assumption Mapping
**Purpose**: Surface and test underlying assumptions
**Process**:
- Identify all assumptions embedded in current approach
- Rank assumptions by criticality and uncertainty
- Design tests for high-risk assumptions
- Plan contingencies for assumption failures
**When to Use**: High uncertainty or innovative approaches

### 15. Scenario Stress Testing
**Purpose**: Test robustness under different conditions
**Process**:
- Define multiple future scenarios (best case, worst case, most likely)
- Test how solution performs under each scenario
- Identify failure points and brittleness
- Design adaptations for different scenarios
**When to Use**: Long-term planning or volatile environments

### 16. Implementation Simulation
**Purpose**: Walk through implementation to identify challenges
**Process**:
- Step through implementation process in detail
- Identify required resources, skills, and dependencies
- Spot potential bottlenecks or failure points
- Refine implementation plan based on simulation
**When to Use**: Complex implementations or resource-constrained environments

## Domain-Specific Methods

### 17. User Journey Mapping
**Purpose**: Evaluate from end-user experience perspective
**Process**:
- Map complete user journey from start to finish
- Identify pain points and friction
- Spot opportunities for improvement
- Design solutions that enhance user experience
**When to Use**: User-facing products or services

### 18. Systems Thinking Analysis
**Purpose**: Understand system-wide implications and feedback loops
**Process**:
- Map system components and relationships
- Identify feedback loops and system dynamics
- Predict unintended consequences
- Design interventions that work with system structure
**When to Use**: Complex systems or organizational changes

### 19. Regulatory Compliance Check
**Purpose**: Ensure approach meets legal and regulatory requirements
**Process**:
- Identify applicable regulations and standards
- Map current approach against requirements
- Identify compliance gaps or risks
- Design compliant alternatives
**When to Use**: Regulated industries or legal compliance required

## Process Improvement Methods

### 20. Lean Analysis
**Purpose**: Eliminate waste and improve efficiency
**Process**:
- Map current process flow
- Identify value-added vs non-value-added activities
- Eliminate waste and bottlenecks
- Streamline for maximum efficiency
**When to Use**: Operational improvements or cost reduction initiatives

### 21. Design Thinking Deep Dive
**Purpose**: Human-centered problem-solving approach
**Process**:
- Empathize with users and stakeholders
- Define core problems to solve
- Ideate multiple solution approaches
- Prototype and test solutions
- Iterate based on feedback
**When to Use**: User-centered design or innovation challenges

## Selection Guidelines

### By Content Type
- **Technical specifications**: Tree of Thoughts, Meta-Prompting, Systems Thinking
- **User requirements**: User Journey Mapping, Stakeholder Roundtable, Design Thinking
- **Strategic plans**: Red Team vs Blue Team, Hindsight Reflection, Scenario Stress Testing
- **Process documentation**: Lean Analysis, Implementation Simulation, Agile Team Perspective

### By Risk Level
- **High risk**: Red Team vs Blue Team, Assumption Mapping, Scenario Stress Testing
- **Medium risk**: Critique and Refine, Identify Potential Risks, Stakeholder Roundtable
- **Low risk**: Expand or Contract for Audience, Assess Alignment with Goals

### By Innovation Need
- **High innovation**: Innovation Tournament, Escape Room Challenge, Design Thinking
- **Medium innovation**: Tree of Thoughts, Meta-Prompting, Systems Thinking
- **Low innovation**: Critique and Refine, Implementation Simulation, Lean Analysis
