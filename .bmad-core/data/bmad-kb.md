# BMad Method Knowledge Base

## Framework Overview

The BMad Method is a specialized framework for AI-assisted business analysis, market research, and strategic planning. It provides structured approaches for conducting research, facilitating brainstorming, and creating comprehensive project documentation.

## Core Principles

### 1. Evidence-Based Analysis
- Ground all findings in verifiable data and credible sources
- Document assumptions and limitations clearly
- Distinguish between facts, hypotheses, and opinions

### 2. Structured Methodology
- Apply systematic methods for thoroughness and consistency
- Use templates and frameworks to ensure comprehensive coverage
- Follow repeatable processes for reliable results

### 3. Collaborative Facilitation
- Guide users to generate their own insights rather than providing solutions
- Use questioning techniques to uncover underlying needs and assumptions
- Facilitate shared understanding among stakeholders

### 4. Action-Oriented Outputs
- Focus on producing actionable deliverables
- Provide clear next steps and recommendations
- Connect analysis to business decisions and outcomes

## Research Methodologies

### Market Research Approaches
- **Primary Research**: Surveys, interviews, focus groups
- **Secondary Research**: Industry reports, academic studies, public data
- **Competitive Intelligence**: Competitor analysis, feature comparison
- **Customer Discovery**: User interviews, journey mapping, needs analysis

### Analysis Frameworks
- **SWOT Analysis**: Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats
- **Porter's Five Forces**: Industry competitive dynamics
- **Jobs-to-be-Done**: Customer outcome-focused analysis
- **Value Proposition Canvas**: Product-market fit assessment

### Validation Techniques
- **Assumption Testing**: Identify and validate key assumptions
- **Prototype Testing**: Early validation with minimal viable products
- **Market Testing**: Small-scale market experiments
- **Stakeholder Validation**: Feedback from key stakeholders

## Documentation Standards

### Template Structure
- **YAML-driven**: Use structured templates for consistency
- **Elicitation Points**: Built-in prompts for deeper exploration
- **Role-based Ownership**: Clear responsibility for different sections
- **Version Control**: Track changes and evolution of documents

### Quality Criteria
- **Clarity**: Accessible to target audience
- **Completeness**: Comprehensive coverage of scope
- **Accuracy**: Factual and up-to-date information
- **Actionability**: Clear next steps and recommendations

## Brainstorming Methodologies

### Divergent Thinking Techniques
- Generate quantity over quality initially
- Defer judgment during idea generation
- Build on others' ideas ("Yes, and...")
- Encourage wild and unconventional ideas

### Convergent Thinking Techniques
- Group and categorize ideas thematically
- Evaluate against defined criteria
- Prioritize based on impact and feasibility
- Synthesize related concepts

### Facilitation Best Practices
- Set clear objectives and constraints
- Use structured techniques appropriately
- Maintain energy and engagement
- Capture all output systematically

## Technology Integration

### AI-Assisted Analysis
- Use AI for data processing and pattern recognition
- Maintain human oversight for interpretation and judgment
- Combine AI efficiency with human creativity and intuition
- Document AI contributions and limitations

### Tool Integration
- Integrate with research databases and sources
- Connect to project management and documentation systems
- Support multiple output formats (markdown, presentations, etc.)
- Enable collaborative editing and review processes
